#  Long term visa 
Article
Stays of more than 90 days require a German national visa. Below you find all information for applying, in more and more cases through the Consular Services Portal  

[ _20.06.2025_ _Article_ Employment, training and jobseeker visa  On this website you find information on visa for working in Germany. The visa categories of Blue Card EU, employment for skilled workers, vocational training, opportunity card, research, self-employment/freelancing, voluntary service, au-pair and recognition measures for foreign qualifications.
