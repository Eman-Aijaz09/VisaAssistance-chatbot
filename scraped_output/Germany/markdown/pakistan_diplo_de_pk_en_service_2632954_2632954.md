#  Opportunity Card (Jobseeking) 
Article
You have a recognized university degree or vocational qualification and would like to find a job in Germany? This page contains information for applications at the German Consulate-General Karachi.   

### Info
This guidance and leaflet only apply to applications at the German Consulate-General in Karachi. Applications at the German Embassy Islamabad are handled via the Consular Services Portal, which contains all guidance and information on required documents. Start your application process [here](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/4-work-jobseeker-1676106 "Employment, training and jobseeker visa"). 
Please familiarize yourself with this visa category and its requirements [HERE](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/jobseekers). Then, please prepare your visa application as explained in [this checklist](https://pakistan.diplo.de/resource/blob/2660740/fbda69ed9a7f88712ee01d161e67cff9/opportunity-card-pdf-data.pdf "OPPORTUNITY CARD PDF").
At a glance, these are the main requirements you have to fulfill:  

  * You hold a university degree which is recognized in Germany (either according to the anabin database or a Statement of Comparability).
  * You have sufficient financial means for your living expenses in Germany in the amount of €1,027 per month.
  * You have sufficient financial means for a return ticket to Pakistan in the amount of €500.
