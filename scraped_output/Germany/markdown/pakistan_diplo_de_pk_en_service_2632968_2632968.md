#  Au-Pair 
Article
You would like to do an au-pair in Germany? This page contains information for applications at the German Consulate-General Karachi.   
  

### Info
This guidance and leaflet only apply to applications at the German Consulate-General in Karachi. Applications at the German Embassy Islamabad are handled via the Consular Services Portal, which contains all guidance and information on required documents. Start your application process [here](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/4-work-jobseeker-1676106 "Employment, training and jobseeker visa"). 
Please familiarize yourself with this visa category and its requirements [HERE](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/unternehmen/arbeitskraefte/au-pair) first. Then, please prepare your visa application as explained in [this checklist](https://pakistan.diplo.de/resource/blob/2583214/b4e496d1ec135ba647a13298289b6d34/au-pair-data.pdf "Au-Pair").
At a glance, these are the main requirements you have to fulfill:
  * You have a valid au-pair contract with a hosting family in Germany.
  * Your hosting family is not of Pakistani origin and consistis of at least one child under the age of 18 years. You are not related to the family.
  * You are between the age of 18 and 27 years old.
  * You have never done an au-pair in Germany before.
  * You have German language proficiency level A1 CEFR.
