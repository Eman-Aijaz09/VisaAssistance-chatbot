#  Research 
Article
You have a hosting agreement or work contract with a research institute in Germany? 
### Info
The following applies **ONLY** to **Research visa** applicants at the **German Embassy in Islamabad:**
Do you wish to take your family with you to Germany? Please indicate accordingly when registering on the appointment waiting list for your Resarch visa application. Your family members may then accompany you on your appointment day and apply for their family reunion visa. Further information about the family reunion visa requirements can be found [HERE](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/1-visa-fz-seite/1676108).
Please familiarize yourself with this visa category and its requirements [HERE](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/other/research) first. Then, please prepare your visa application as explained in [this checklist](https://pakistan.diplo.de/resource/blob/2632960/8c805e1694edb4f99e6c237eae8d849f/research-1--data.pdf "Research").
At a glance, these are the main requirements you have to fulfill:
  * You have a hosting agreement or work contract with a research institute in Germany.
  * You have sufficient financial means for your living expenses in Germany in the amount of €1,027 per month.
