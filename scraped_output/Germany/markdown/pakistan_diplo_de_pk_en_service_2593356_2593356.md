#  EU Blue Card 
Article
You have a recognized university degree and already found a job in Germany? This page contains information for applications at the German Consulate-General Karachi.   

### Info
This guidance and leaflet only apply to applications at the German Consulate-General in Karachi. Applications at the German Embassy Islamabad are handled via the Consular Services Portal, which contains all guidance and information on required documents. Start your application process [here](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/4-work-jobseeker-1676106 "Employment, training and jobseeker visa"). 
Please familiarize yourself with this visa category and its requirements [HERE](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/eu-blue-card) first. Then, please prepare your visa application as explained in [this checklist](https://pakistan.diplo.de/resource/blob/2582880/797f5cdeb94793eaab61aa14ab185c47/eu-blue-card-pdf-data.pdf "EU Blue Card").  

At a glance, these are the main requirements you have to fulfill:
  * You have a valid employment contract with a company in Germany.
  * You hold a university degree that is recognized in Germany (either according to the anabin database or a Statement of Comparability).
  * You fulfill **ONE** of the following:
    * Your yearly gross salary amounts to minimum €48,300 (as per 2025) OR
    * Your yearly gross salary amounts to minimum €43,759.80 (as per 2025) PLUS you have earned your university degree within the last three years OR
    * Your yearly gross salary amounts to minimum €43,759.80 (as per 2025) PLUS you are going to be employed in an understaffed profession (e.g. IT, STEM). A list can be found [HERE](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/pdf-engpassberufe-en).


### Info
If you do not meet the requirements for “EU Blue Card”, please check if you might qualify for another category [“Employment for Skilled Workers Holding a University Degree (NON EU Blue Card)”](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/-/2595682)
