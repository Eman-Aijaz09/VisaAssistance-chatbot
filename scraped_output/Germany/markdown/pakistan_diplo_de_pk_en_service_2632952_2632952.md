#  Vocational Training 
Article
You have found a vocational training programme in Germany? This page contains information for applications at the German Consulate-General Karachi.   

### Info
This guidance and leaflet only apply to applications at the German Consulate-General in Karachi. Applications at the German Embassy Islamabad are handled via the Consular Services Portal, which contains all guidance and information on required documents. Start your application process [here](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/4-work-jobseeker-1676106 "Employment, training and jobseeker visa"). 
For the time being, applications for vocational training visa in Karachi will continue to be made in person at the Consulate General. 
The requirements for applications in Karachi can be found in [this checklist](https://pakistan.diplo.de/resource/blob/2582912/4fe4f0169c1c7e408af7a755e1b50880/vocational-training-data.pdf "Vocational Training").
At a glance, these are the main requirements you have to fulfill:
  * You have a valid vocational training contract with a company in Germany.
  * You have German language proficiency level B1 CEFR.
