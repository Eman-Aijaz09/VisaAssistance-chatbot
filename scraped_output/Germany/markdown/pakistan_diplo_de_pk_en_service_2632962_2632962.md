#  Self-Employment, Freelancing 
Article
You would like to start a business or work as a freelancer in Germany? 
Please familiarize yourself with this visa category and its requirements [HERE](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/self-employment) first. Please prepare your visa application as explained in [this checklist](https://pakistan.diplo.de/resource/blob/2582906/c9b498a6334f5526371b8b85ab4efc2f/self-employment-freelancing-mb-data.pdf "Self-Employment, Freelancing").
At a glance, these are the main requirements you have to fulfill:
  * Your business idea meets an economic interest or a regional need and is expected to have positive effects on the economy in Germany.
  * You have personal capital or a loan undertaking to realize the business idea.
