#  Employment for Skilled Workers with Vocational Qualification 
Article
You have a recognized vocational qualification and already found a job in Germany? This page contains information for applications at the German Consulate-General Karachi.   

### Info
This guidance and leaflet only apply to applications at the German Consulate-General in Karachi. Applications at the German Embassy Islamabad are handled via the Consular Services Portal, which contains all guidance and information on required documents. Start your application process [here](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/4-work-jobseeker-1676106 "Employment, training and jobseeker visa"). 
Please familiarize yourself with this visa category and its requirements [HERE](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/work-qualified-professionals). Then, please prepare your visa application as explained in [this checklist.](https://pakistan.diplo.de/resource/blob/2583210/66e29290d2aec4263873c318f7c4a560/sw-voc-qual-data.pdf "Employment for Skilled Workers with Vocational Qualification")
At a glance, these are the main requirements you have to fulfill:
  * You have a valid employment contract with a company in Germany.
  * You have a vocational qualification which is recognized by the competent German authority.
