International Travel Guidance
Your safety is our top priority when you travel abroad. This guide makes it easy for you to find important safety travel basics as you plan for your trip.
Preparing to visit your destination
Review the current Travel Advisory, entry requirements, local laws, and tips from the U.S. embassy or consulate for the destination you plan to visit. You should also enroll in the Smart Traveler Enrollment Program (STEP) before you go.
Traveling abroad involves many important safety considerations. Our resources for different travelers' personal needs or safety tips for activities, can help you make preparations for your trip.
