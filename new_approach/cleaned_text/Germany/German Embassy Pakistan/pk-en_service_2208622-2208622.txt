Abolition of Remonstration Procedure
Up to 30th June 2025, the Federal Foreign Office has provided a voluntary objection procedure for an initial refusal letter.
Starting on 1st July 2025, the remonstration procedure for visa rejections is cancelled indefinitely and withouth prejudice.
The processing of a greater number of visa applications and a reduction in waiting times benefits all applicants as a direct result for the procedure demanded a high amount of resources.
Processing Times
For Schengen visa application the processing time is usually fourteen (14) calendar days starting at initial application.
All national visa application processing times highly vary and depend on the reason of long-term stay as well as the quality of the application and submitted supporting documents.
