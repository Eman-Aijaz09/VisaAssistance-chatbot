Make it in Germany
If your are interested in working and living in Germany, you find extensive information on the Make it in Germany web page.
The page includes information on the legal bases of employment visa, routes to have your Pakistani qualification recognised and a large database of job listings.
Please consult this web page carefully before launching your application - good preparation will save you time afterwards.
German Missions in Pakistan: Apply online via the Consular Services Portal
The following categories can apply through our Online Service Portal:
- Blue Card EU (including your family reunion visa!)
- Skilled workers with an academic background (University degree)
- Skilled workers with vocational education
- Skilled workers with practical experience (a minimum of two years in experience during the last five years, vocational training obtained in your home country)
- Au Pair: please use the categories for skilled workers
- Specialty Cooks (with a track record of a minimum of 6 years of experience as a “chef”): please use the category for skilled workers with practical experience
- Vocational Training
- Recognition Measurments (e.g. medical personal, physical therapist): please use the category “Qualification Analysis”
- Opportunity Cards
Priority processing with § 81a pre-approval letter
Applicants with a pre-approval letter not older than three month have the possibility for expedited processing.
- Register on our priority lists for § 81a pre-approvals. You find the priority list under this link.
- AND register on the Consular Services Portal to upload your application
+++NEW+++ Family Reunion applications for Blue Card EU, Researchers or Priority processing
The online portal allows you to attach your family reunion applications simultaniously to your own.
Please note:
Complete your application in the Services Portal and attach all family reunion applications of your spouse and children. Please follow the guidelines and information of family reunion page.
Research, voluntary service and self employment
For technical reasons, research visa applications, as well as applications for self employment and voluntary service in Germany can at this point not be launched on the Consular Services Portal.
Please register for an appointment in the regular way and bring all required documents to your appointment in a well-ordered manner.
Please make sure to bring all required documents with you and be on time on your application day. Further information about how to access the Embassy can be found at this link.
