There are a number of agencies that claim to offer support in booking appointments or preparing your applications. You should not trust them.

Applications and bookings at the German Missions in Pakistan are simple and do not require the support of agencies. The success of your application depends solely on your submitted documents.

Agencies or consultants can never provide you with visa appointments or earlier appointments, or enhance your chances of success. If any agent or consultant claims to do this, this is a fraudulent claim.

The German Missions in Pakistan reserve the right to cancel any appointment booked through an agent or consultant without warning.

Consular Services

Below you have access to all consular services of the German Missions in Pakistan.

Most services of the German Missions must be booked online. For some categories, there are waiting lists. Please browse through the categories below to inform yourself of the booking for your respective category to ensure you register correctly and come to your appointment well prepared. Please do not try to book in a different category because you believe that this will lead to a quicker appointment. Applicants in the wrong category will be sent away.

Visa applications: To determine the right category for your visa application, use the Visa Navigator.

With regard to visa for Afghan applicants, please refer to the website of the German Embassy Kabul https://afghanistan.diplo.de/af-en/kontakt-konsulat
