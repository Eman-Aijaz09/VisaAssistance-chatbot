    Points of contact for qualified professionals and their families in the federal states

    We are pleased to welcome you to Germany. You being able to settle into your new home country easily is something we put great importance on. That is the reason why, on this page, you will be able to find contacts details of local contact persons, who will support you during the process. The service points available to you vary depending on the federal state as well as the region.

    The services offered at the Welcome Centers are geared towards foreign qualified workers. If you are looking for advice on the recognition of qualifications, reach out to the Network IQ, for example. The local Chambers of Industry and Commerce as well as the universities’ International Offices offer services for those who are interested in vocational training or academic studies.

    Federal state

    German: Bundesland

    A federal state (often called “Land”) is a constituent state with its own parliament (“Landtag”), its own constitution and government, and, in some cases, its own laws. In accordance with the concept of federalism, the exercise of all state authority is divided between the Federal Government and the federal states. The Federal Republic of Germany consists of 16 federal states.
