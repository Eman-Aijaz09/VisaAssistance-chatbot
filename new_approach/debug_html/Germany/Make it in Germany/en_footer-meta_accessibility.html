    Accessibility statement

    Introduction

    The Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy is committed to making the website www.make-it-in-germany.com accessible. The legal basis for this is the Act on Equal Opportunities of Persons with Disabilities (BGG) (External link)  and the Accessibility in Information Technology Ordinance (BITV) (External link) .

    Status of compliance with requirements

    This website is partially compliant with the requirements of BITV 2.0. This assessment is based on a self-assessment completed on 16 September 2025. 

    Non-accessible content

    Non-accessible parts of the website are the following:

    • 9.1.1.1a Alternative texts for control elements
      • Alternative texts for graphics and objects may sometimes be misleading.
    • 9.1.1.1b Alternative texts for graphics and objects
      • Information in the alternative text is incomplete or unnecessary. The image shows more than is described in the alt attribute, or the image is purely decorative.
      • Alternative texts are partially missing.
    • 9.1.2.3 Audio description or full-text alternative for videos
      • No audio description or full-text alternatives available.
    • External videos
      • Externally embedded videos from third-party providers may not be fully accessible. In particular, subtitles, audio descriptions or other accessible alternatives may be missing.
        We have no direct influence on the accessibility in regards to the design of this content, as it is provided by external providers. Offering fully accessible edits is a disproportionate burden at this point in time.
    • 9.1.3.1a HTML structural elements for headings
      • Headings are not always correctly marked up with the intended HTML structural elements h1 to h6.
    • Missing accessibility information in PDF files
      • The PDF files created via the website or offered for download are not accessible.The documents concerned are listed below.
