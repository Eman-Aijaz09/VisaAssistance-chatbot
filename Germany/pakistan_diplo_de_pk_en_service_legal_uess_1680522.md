#  Legal 
14.04.2026 - Article 
Notarizations, “Lebensbescheinigungen” and more 
### Info
**Please note:** Documents issued by Pakistani authorities, such as birth certificates or marriage registration certificates cannot be attested or legalized by the German Embassy. Please find below the document (in German) outlining the procedure for such requirements. 
The German Embassy in Islamabad and the German Consulate in Karachi are authorized to do notarizations, attestations, certification of documents and signatures as well as to issue certificates in certain cases.Find more information about the administrative / consular district which is responsible for your request, necessary documents for your appointment, fees and how to book an appointment in our leaflet. 
## Certified copies of study documents 
If you need certified copies of your school/university/education documents in order to apply at German University, you can get this done at our embassy in Islamabad and our consulate general in Karachi. 
At the German Embassy Islamabad appointments can only be booked online ([Online Appointments Embassy Islamabad](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/<https:/service2.diplo.de/rktermin/extern/choose_realmList.do?locationCode=isla&request_locale=en> "Online appointment for the Embassy in Islamabad")).At the German Consulate General Karachi, no appointment is required, you can come from Monday to Thursday between 1:30 and 2:30pm. 
Please submit your original certificates along with corresponding copies (no cross attestation by the HEC Pakistan required) and your passport in the same order. Only a maximum of 4 sets of copies can be attested per person and day. 
In order to obtain one set of certified documents for free, please establish proof of your intention to study in Germany, e.g. by submitting a copy of an e-mail of the German university. 
If you reside in or close to Lahore, please contact our **Honorary Consul Mr. Arif Saeed** for an attestation of your certificates.

## Translations
There are **no** sworn translators in Paksitan.
You can find a translator in Germany via the [Databank of interpreters and translators of the individual states of the Federal Republic of Germany (in German and English)](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/<https:/www.justiz-dolmetscher.de/Recherche/>). The Embassy can give no guarantee for the content, quality or cost of translations.
## Blocked Account Release Letters (Consular Attestation)
In case you have already applied for a study visa or you are on our waiting list and do not wish to pursue your application, the Embassy may issue a consular attestation confirming that you have not been, or will not be granted a study visa. This letter may also be issued if your visa application was refused by the Embassy. 
In any case, we recommend prior contact to your bank, to confirm that this Consular Attestation is necessary.
Please bring the following documents to your appointment:
• Valid passport• Confirmation letter for your blocked account• Fee (cash in PKR) for issuing the Blocked Account Release Letter (36€; around 12,000 PKR depending on the current exchange rate of the Embassy) • Visa refusal letter (if applicable)• Proof of an expired/cancelled visa (if applicable).• withdrawn admission letter (certificate of registration) from the University in Germany (if applicable)
Once you have prepared the necessary documents you may book an appointment via this [Online-LINK. ](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/<https:/service2.diplo.de/rktermin/extern/choose_realmList.do?locationCode=isla&request_locale=en>)
