#  Recognition Measure for a Foreign Qualification 
Article 
You have an assessment note by a competent authority in Germany and would like your professional qualification to be recognized? This page contains information for applications at the German Consulate-General Karachi. 
### Info
This guidance and leaflet only apply to applications at the German Consulate-General in Karachi. Applications at the German Embassy Islamabad are handled via the Consular Services Portal, which contains all guidance and information on required documents. Start your application process [here](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</pk-en/service/4-work-jobseeker-1676106> "Employment, training and jobseeker visa"). 
Please familiarize yourself with this visa category and its requirements [HERE](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/<https:/www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/types/recognition>) first. Then, please prepare your visa application as explained in [this checklist](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</resource/blob/2635190/5016b8447b6eaefd6fdee29401c1f711/recognition-measure-for-a-foreign-qualification-1--data.pdf> "Recognition Measure for a Foreign Qualification"). 
At a glance, these are the main requirements you have to fulfill: 
  * You have an assessment note (“Defizitbescheid”, “Zwischenbescheid”) by a competent authority in Germany.
  * You have found a suitable qualification program in Germany.
  * You have German language proficiency level A2 CEFR.
