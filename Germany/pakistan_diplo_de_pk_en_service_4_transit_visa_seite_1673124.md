#  Transit visa 
![Frankfurt Airport](https://pakistan.diplo.de/resource/blob/292/1d405f76d7ae8e678c7864e62790f650/loading-data.gif)
Frankfurt Airport
Image information 
Article 
Pakistani nationals in many cases require a visa even for the international transit area of a German airport. You can apply for a transit visa as set out below. 
### Which visa is required?
#### Full Schengen visa
A transit visa will only allow you to stay within the transit area of a German airport. Transit areas are not available at every airport. In general, only Frankfurt Main and Munich airport have transit areas that are open 24 hours. For other airports, or if you transit between airports in Europe, you will require a regular Schengen visa. Please refer to the guidance for [tourist](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</pk-en/service/3-tourist-visa-seite-1673122> "Tourist visa"), [visit ](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</pk-en/service/2-visitors-visa-seite-1673128> "Visitor visa")or [business visa](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</pk-en/service/1673894-1673894> "Business visa"). 
#### No transit visa required
No airport transit visa might be required for **holders of diplomatic passports** , **family members** of an** EU or UK citizen** or people who hold any of the following:
  * a residence permit of a Schengen state, a non-Schengen EU state, Andorra, Canada, Japan, San Marino, the United Kingdom, the United States of America or a Dutch Overseas Territory;
  * a visa for a Schengen state, a non-Schengen EU state, Canada, Japan, the United States of America or a Dutch Overseas Territory, for travels with that country.


### In your own interest, please take care when preparing your application
**Include in your applications all the documents listed below, in exactly the way described and in correct order.**
If you neglect to provide any document, **your application can be immediately rejected**. At the least, you will introduce delays as processing your application only starts once you have submitted it in full. 
The Visa Section can at any time request any additional document from you. Your application is only complete if you have complied with the document request. 
Copies must be in A4 format.
### Required documents
**1**| **Your current passport and copies of all previous passports and visa** | **Original** of current passport and **copies** of pages 1 and 2Previous passports in **full copy** Your passport needs to be valid for at least three months after the end of the desired visa.All passports must be signed by the holder. Unsigned passports are invalid under Pakistani law and cannot receive visa. You can only be considered for a multiple entry visa of longer duration, if your passport has a long additional validity.If any of your previous passports has been lost, provide a police report with English or German translation. If you have received any visa on paper (not in your passport), please also provide a copy.   
---|---|---  
**2**| **Videx visa application form**| **Signed original, printed from the system.** You must fill out your application on the [Videx online application system](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/<https:/videx.diplo.de/videx/visum-erfassung/videx-kurzfristiger-aufenthalt> "Videx"), then print out and sign.   
**3**| **Two biometrical Passport Photos**| **Original** Please follow the [examples of passport photographs](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</resource/blob/1673176/6e3eee9fd4d86e16aaefe0e92d809332/photos-samples-data.pdf> "information on photos")The photographs must not be older than six months.One photograp must be stapled on the application form and a further photograph attached with a paper clip.  
**4**| **Health insurance policy**| **Signed original and one copy** A [list of health insurance companies](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/<https:/pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/6-health-insurances-seite/1673134>) in Pakistan that comply with Schengen standards can be found at the link. Your minimum insurance cover must be € 30000. Your policy must cover all Schengen countries not only Germany.  
**5**| **Flight reservation**| **Copy / printout** Please provide **confirmed round trip tickets** covering the duration of your stay.  
**6**| **All previous visa refusals** | **Copy** Checks are run against the system for any refusals. Failure to disclose a refusal contitutes fraud and is an automatic grounds for refusing the visa.   
**7**| **ID card (CNIC)**| **Copy**  
**8**| **FRC**(Family Registration Certificate)**Marriage Certificate**(if applicable), **Birth Certificate (** for minor applicants)| **Original** Certificates are provided by NADRA in English language  
**9**| **List of children**| **Signed original** You can download a template to complete [here](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</resource/blob/2448694/763fe5f1db602e11245d926d662688b9/list-of-children-data.pdf> "List of Children")  
**10**| **List of relatives living abroad**| **Signed original** You can download a template to complete [here](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</resource/blob/2368274/53f6e169ee3c1b43ada4fdbf441e4480/list-of-relatives-residing-outside-of-pakistan-data.pdf> "List of Relatives outside of Pakistan")  
**11**| **Appointment email**| **Printout** You have received the appointment email to your email address registered with the Embassy. Your printout must show that email address.
