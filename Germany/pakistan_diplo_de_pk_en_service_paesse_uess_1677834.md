07.06.2024 - Article 
Would you like to apply for a German passport or ID card for yourself or your child? 
### Info
Please note that a child may not have acquired German citizenship at birth if one parent was born after 01.01.2000. Should you require further information regarding the generational cut-off point, please refer to the[ linked page.](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</pk-en/2775158-2775158> "Nationality \(The Generational Cut-off Point\)")
We will show you how to apply for a German ID document in five simple steps.
### **Step 1: Prepare your application**
You can only submit your application **in person and in writing** to one of the two German missions. To prepare, please download the correct application form, fill it out, print it out and then sign it by hand.
  * [Form for **passport** applicants under18 years old and **ID card** applicants under 16 years old](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</resource/blob/2660792/35ba82592942338cefe6917e82c42005/paspport-application-form-minors-data.pdf> "Application form MINORS")
  * [Form for **passport** applicants over18 years old and **ID card** applicants over 16 years old](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</resource/blob/2660794/b48d9a738a74716e569053a384be52b2/paspport-application-form-adults-data.pdf> "Application form ADULTS")


Please then obtain all other application documents required for your application **in the original or as a photocopy officially attested by a German authority.**
  * [Checklist](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</resource/blob/2661058/16da651e84f56c9322016e023891f3b3/chechliste-pass-data.pdf> "Checkliste Reisepass- und Personalausweisanträge")


### **Step 2: Make an appointment**
Please make an appointment with the mission that is responsible for your application.
  * Do you live in **Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan or Azad Jammu and Kashmir**? Then the **German Embassy in Islamabad** is responsible for your application. Please make an appointment [**HERE**](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/<https:/service2.diplo.de/rktermin/extern/choose_categoryList.do?locationCode=isla&realmId=190&request_locale=en>).
  * Do you live in **Sindh or Balochistan**? Then the **German Consulate General in Karachi** is responsible for your application. Please make an appointment at **+92 21 3587 3782 or +92 21 3582 1596**.


### **Step 3: Submit your application**
Please bring your prepared application documents with you to your appointment. There are fees and surcharges for processing your application. These must be paid **in Pakistani rupees (according to the mission’s exchange rate) and in cash** when you submit your application. Therefore, please bring a sufficient amount of cash with you. ### Info
**For first-time applicants born in Pakistan: 75,000 PKR fee for verification of Pakistani documents by a mission's cooperation lawyer**
When you submit an application, we must check the data you have provided (name, dates of birth, parentage). For this purpose, you are obliged to support your data with sufficient evidence. In most cases, this is only possible by submitting civil status documents (especially birth and marriage certificates)
Documents issued by Pakistani authorities are generally only valid for the German legal system if they have been checked by one of our cooperation lawyers with regard to authenticity and accuracy of content. **An apostille or legalization process is not possible.**
The verification procedure takes **at least seven months** from the time the lawyer is commissioned until the result of the verification is received. There is no possibility of speeding up this procedure. Unfortunately, we are unable to respond to status update inquiries within the first seven months. We will notify you by e-mail as soon as the review result is available.
**There is a fee of PKR 75,000 PKR for the lawyer's services. This amount must be paid in cash as a security deposit directly at the time of your application.** We will only pay your security deposit to the lawyer once we have received the result of the review.
### **Step 4: Wait for our notification**
Once you have submitted your application, please be patient. We will check your application. We may find that documents are missing and ask you to submit them later. You will receive a notification e-mail from us when you can collect your passport/ID card.
### Info
**Processing times**
Once you have submitted your application, we will check your application as quickly as possible. We may find that documents are missing and ask you to submit them later.
If a document verification by a cooperation lawyer is necessary (usually always when born in Pakistan and applying for an ID document for the first time), this will take**at least seven months**. If the result shows that a document is faulty or incorrect, we will not be able to issue an ID document for the time being. Instead, you are then required to correct the document(s) and resubmit your corrected documents. A new document verification may be necessary, which will again take **at least seven months**.
If a name declaration is necessary (usually always when born in Pakistan and applying for an ID document for the first time), this will take **at least two months**. 
Only when we have fully checked your application and all requirements for issuing have been met, we will commission Bundedruckerei GmbH in Berlin to print your ID document. The production time is **approximately seven to ten working days**. If you have selected the express procedure when submitting your application, the production time is reduced to three working days.
As soon as your ID document has been printed, Bundesdruckerei GmbH will send it to the Federal Foreign Office in Berlin. From there, it is then sent by official courier to the German Embassy in Islamabad or the German Consulate General in Karachi. The dispatch takes **about seven working days**.
Once we have received your ID document, we will notify you by e-mail. You can then collect it at any time.
### **Step 5: Collect your ID document**
Please collect your passport/ID card from us once you have received our collection notification by e-mail. You or an authorized representative can only collect your ID document **in person**. We cannot send your ID document to you by post. **If you still have an old ID document, please bring it with you when you collect it so that we can invalidate it. If you forget your old ID document, we will unfortunately not be able to give you your new ID document.**
### Info
**Collection from our Honorary Consul in Lahore**
We can also send your ID document to the German Honorary Consul in Lahore for collection, if Lahore is easier for you to reach. If you are interested in this offer, please mention it when submitting your application.
