#  Employment for Speciality Cook 
Article 
You would like to work as a Speciality Cook in Germany? This page contains information for applications at the German Consulate-General Karachi. 
### Info
This guidance and leaflet only apply to applications at the German Consulate-General in Karachi. Applications at the German Embassy Islamabad are handled via the Consular Services Portal, which contains all guidance and information on required documents. Start your application process [here](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</pk-en/service/4-work-jobseeker-1676106> "Employment, training and jobseeker visa"). 
Please prepare your visa application as explained in [this checklist](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</resource/blob/2583216/3f86f6e20ae27ad4a48129420e2f6e7a/specialty-cook-data.pdf> "Specialty Cook").
At a glance, these are the main requirements you have to fulfill:
  * You have a valid employment contract with a Pakistani restaurant in Germany.
  * You have six years of professional experience as a Pakistani cook.
