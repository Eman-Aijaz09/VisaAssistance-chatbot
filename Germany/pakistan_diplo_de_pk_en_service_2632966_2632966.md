#  Voluntary Service 
Article 
You would like to do a voluntary service in Germany? 
### Info
These are the different categories of voluntary services in Germany:
- Federal Voluntary Service (Bundesfreiwilligendienst, BFD)
- Voluntary Social Year (Freiwilliges Soziales Jahr, FSJ)
- Voluntary Ecological Year (Freiwilliges Ökologisches Jahr, FÖJ)
- European Voluntary Service (Europäischer Freiwilligendienst, EFD)
Please prepare your visa application as explained in [this checklist](https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/</resource/blob/2583212/d86a251c31462849392b2a6b599ad38a/voluntary-service-data.pdf> "Voluntary Service").
At a glance, this is the main requirement you have to fulfill:
  * You have a valid contract with a voluntary service provider in Germany.
