Putting American Travelers First

The U.S. Department of State has no higher priority than the safety and security of Americans. The Bureau of Consular Affairs issues U.S. passports, informs and assists Americans abroad, and strengthens America's security and safety by adjudicating and rigorously screening visa applications for travel to the United States.

Emergency helplines

Americans in need of assistance abroad may contact the nearest U.S. embassy(opens in a new tab) or call us 24/7:

Get embassy updates right to your inbox

Smart Traveler Enrollment Program (STEP) is a free service for Americans. It enables the Department of State to quickly contact you in case of an emergency. You will also get important updates and alerts from the U.S. embassy or consulate for the destination you are visiting or living in.
