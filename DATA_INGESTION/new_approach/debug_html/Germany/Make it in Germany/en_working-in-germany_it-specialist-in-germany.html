    The "Make IT in Germany" partner network

    Bitkom e.V.

    Bitkom is Germany’s digital association and represents more than 2,300 companies in the digital economy. Their goal is to establish Germany as a key driver of digital change in Europe and on a global level.

    Go to website

    de:hub initiative

    The de:hub initiative is a nationwide network for the promotion of new technologies. The initiative connects SMEs with international companies, investors and experts from the start-up scene and science at 25 locations in Germany.

    Go to website

    Bundesverband Deutsche Startups e.V.

    The German network supports start-ups throughout Germany. It shares experiences, imparts knowledge and represents the interests of start-ups to society. The network connects SMEs and corporations with start-ups.
