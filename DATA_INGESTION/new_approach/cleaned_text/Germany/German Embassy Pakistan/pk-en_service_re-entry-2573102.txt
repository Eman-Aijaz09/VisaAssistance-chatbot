Welcome
If you residence permit expired or was stolen during your stay in Pakistan, you need a Visa to re-enter Germany.
Please note that you must present your original documents when applying for the visa (including your passports). The passport you intend to travel to Germany on will remain at the foreign mission during the processing time.
The diplomatic mission reserves the right to contact you even after the application has been submitted to request additional documents.

German Embassy Islamabad: Please make sure you have all necessary documents and then contact the visa section of the embassy via e-mail in order to get an appointment.
German Consulate General Karachi: Please make sure you have all necessary documents and then contact the visa section of the embassy via e-mail or telephone in order to get an appointment.
