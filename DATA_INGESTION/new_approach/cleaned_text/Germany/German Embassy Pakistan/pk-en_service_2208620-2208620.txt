Information for applicants from Afghanistan

Applicants for long-term visa applications (such as family reunion etc.) and Schengen visa applicants, who reside in Afghanistan and fall under the regional consular jurisdiction of the German Embassy Kabul, can file their visa applications at the German Embassy Islamabad starting 18th September 2017. The registration for an appointment is only possible via the website of the German Embassy Kabul. Registrations/ booking of appointments via the website of the German Embassy Islamabad cannot be accepted and will be cancelled. It is not possible to file a visa application without prior appointment. Further information regarding the application process (also regarding documents to be presented) can be found on the website of the German Embassy Kabul:

Visa applications from Afghan applicants are accepted at the German Embassy Islamabad. The address is as follows: German Embassy Islamabad, Ramna 5, Diplomatic Enclave, Islamabad, Pakistan.
