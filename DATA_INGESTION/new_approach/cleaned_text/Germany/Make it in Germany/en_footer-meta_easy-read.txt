How this Web page works
You are on the “Make it in Germany” Web site.
“Make it in Germany” means:
“Be successful (do well) in Germany”.
On this page, we will tell you
how the Web site works.
The Home page shows important topics. The Make it in Germany logo is on the right-hand side at the top.  

You can click the logo. It will always take you back to the Home page.
Above the logo you can select the language.
You can click in English, German, Spanish or French. 
If you click on the globe
the other short language versions will open.
To the left of the language selection is: "For skilled workers" and "For employers".
Click on "For skilled workers".
Then you get info for people who are looking for a job in Germany.
If you click on "For employers",
you will get information for companies.
To the left of the logo is the search.
If you do not find an info,
you can search for it.
Click on the magnifying glass in the search field,
write the word you are looking for in the field
and then click on the magnifying glass.
Or press the Enter key on your keyboard.
Then a list will appear.
In the list are the informations that match the searched word.
To the left of the search are icons for
Contact form, Hotline, Chat and FAQ.
At the top of the start page you will see a line with six topics:
- Working in Germany

- Living in Germany
- Service
- About us

Or move the pointer of the mouse on a word.

a new page opens.

What is Make it in Germany?
Make it in Germany is the German government’s portal.
The Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy is responsible for the portal.
Make it in Germany wants:
That people in other countries
find Germany good.
People with a good education should see:
You can work and live well in Germany.
The Web site has lots of information about Germany.
It tells you about jobs.
On the page are:
- Infomations about Germany
- Videos.
In the videos people who work in Germany talk
The people in the videos
have a good formation.
The page shows:
Job offers.
Germany is a good country for all people.
Make it in Germany collaborates with:
- the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy
- the Federal Ministry of Labour and Social Affairs
- the Federal Employment Agency
- the Federal Foreign Office
- the Federal Ministry of the Interior
- the Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space
- the Federal Office for Migration and Refugees
How can I write to Make it in Germany?
You can write a message to Make it in Germany.
Click here
A new site will pop up.
The new site has different boxes
You can type in those boxes.
The form has different boxes with a * next to some of them.
You must fill in all the boxes with a star next to them.
That means: you must write your name
in the Name box.
You must write your e-mail address in the E-mail address box.
You must write your country of origin in the Country of origin field.
You must choose a subject in the Subject box.
Click on the subject of your choice.
There are other boxes too.
For example, you can enter your profession and age.
That will help us to send you more information.
Then, in the Message box, write your message to Make it in Germany.
The Privacy Policy field must be clicked.
When you have finished writing the message, click Send now.
Your message will then be sent to Make it in Germany.
We are looking forward to your message.
