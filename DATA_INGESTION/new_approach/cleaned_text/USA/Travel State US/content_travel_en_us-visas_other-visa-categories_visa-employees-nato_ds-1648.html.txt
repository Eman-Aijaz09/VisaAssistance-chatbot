Welcome to the DS-1648: Online Application for A, G, or NATO Visa (Applying in the United States only).
Important Notice: The online form DS-1648 available from the Department of State, at https://ceac.state.gov/agnato/, is the only acceptable version of this form. Only online (internet-based) applications are accepted for those eligible to use this form. Applicants are cautioned that any other version of the DS-1648 found on other websites is not acceptable.
