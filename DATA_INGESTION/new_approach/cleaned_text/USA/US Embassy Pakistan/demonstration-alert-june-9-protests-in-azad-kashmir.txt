Location: Pakistan
Event: Demonstration Alert
We are monitoring reports of a planned protest called by the Joint Awami Action Committee (JAAC) across Azad Kashmir on June 9. Local authorities issued a travel warning deterring all tourism from the area from June 5 until June 20. JAAC has previously led large-scale protests in the region, periodically bringing parts of Azad Kashmir to a standstill. The party plans a long march on June 9 starting in Rawalakot and moving to the capital, Muzaffarabad.
We advise U.S. citizens in Azad Kashmir to monitor local news, observe good personal security practices, including being aware of your surroundings, avoiding large crowds, and ensuring your STEP registration is up to date.
Actions to Take:
- Avoid areas of large public gatherings.
- Review your personal security plans.
- Monitor local media for updates.
- Monitor email and local media for further updates regarding appointments at all locations.
- Keep a low profile.
- Carry identification and cooperate with police.
