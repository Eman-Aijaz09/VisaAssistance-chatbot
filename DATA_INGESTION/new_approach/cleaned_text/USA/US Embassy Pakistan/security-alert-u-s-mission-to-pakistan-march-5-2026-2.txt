Location: Islamabad
Event: The U.S. government cares about your safety and will continue to share up-to-the-minute information you need to make informed decisions about your safety. Due to possible protests in throughout Pakistan on Friday, March 6 the movements of all U.S. personnel have been limited.
We advise U.S. citizens in Pakistan to continue to monitor local news, observe good personal security practices, including being aware of your surroundings, avoiding large crowds, and ensuring your STEP registration is up to date.
Embassy Islamabad Operations
The Consular Section will not be open for routine services on March 6 but staff remain available for emergency services.
Actions to Take:
- Avoid areas of large public gatherings.
- Review your personal security plans.
- Monitor local media for updates.
- Monitor email and local media for further updates regarding appointments at all locations.
- Keep a low profile.
- Carry identification and cooperate with police.
