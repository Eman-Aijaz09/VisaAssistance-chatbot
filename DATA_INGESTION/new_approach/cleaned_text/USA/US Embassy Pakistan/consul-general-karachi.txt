Charles Goodman, a career member of the Senior Foreign Service, is currently serving as the U.S. Consul General in Karachi, Pakistan.
Previously, Charles was the Management Counselor for the U.S. Mission to New Zealand and Samoa, and he served as Management Counselor and Acting Deputy Chief of Mission in Pristina, Kosovo. In other assignments, Charles led the General Services team in the Executive Office of the Secretary, and he served as Deputy Director of the Narcotics and Law Enforcement Affairs Section in Lima, Peru. His earlier postings included Latvia, Georgia, Pakistan, and Argentina.
Charles joined the U.S. Foreign Service in 2006 following 20 years in the private sector, where he held leadership roles in financial services, real estate, and technology fields.
Charles has an MBA from Duke University’s Fuqua School of Business, and a B.S. in Management Information Systems from the University of Tampa. Charles speaks Spanish, and at various points in his career has been proficient in Latvian and French.
