Location: Islamabad
Event: Security Alert
Following reports of a suicide bombing at a mosque in the Tarlai Kalan area on the outskirts of Islamabad, the United States Embassy reminds you to take the following precautions when traveling in Pakistan:
- Actions to Take:
- Avoid areas of large public gatherings.
- Review your personal security plans.
- Monitor local media for updates.
- Keep a low profile.
- Carry identification and cooperate with police.
