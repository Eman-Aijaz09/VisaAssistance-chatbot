Notice:
We require an appointment for all routine services except emergencies. American citizen services staff MAY NOT answer visa questions. If you have a visa/green card inquiry, please check with our visa section.
Appointment Rules for Islamabad
The U.S. Embassy is located in Islamabad’s Diplomatic Enclave.
When you go in person to the U.S. embassy or consulate for an ACS appointment, you may bring someone else with you (for example, an attorney, interpreter, caregiver, or family member) to provide assistance. All visitors to an embassy or consulate will be subject to standard security procedures and screening.
- Attendance by an attorney and/or other third party at the appointment does not excuse the personal appearance of the applicant or the applicant’s parent/legal guardian as otherwise required.
- Expenses related to third party attendance are solely the responsibility of the applicant.
- Attendees may take written notes but may not otherwise record the appointment.
Applicants using a DS-82:
To renew an adult passport should use the mail-in renewal program and pay the DS- 82 passport application fee via the U.S. government’s secure payment site . Paying online allows you to mail your application to the U.S. Embassy or Consulate without the need to pay in person. Find out more here by clicking “How to mail-in your adult passport renewal from anywhere in Pakistan.”
Forms:
To avoid inconvenience and delay in processing, please download the required form(s) and complete them before coming to the Embassy. Make sure you bring the completed forms and supporting documents on the day of your appointment. Applicants with incomplete or no forms will be requested to reschedule.
Passport Fees
Form of payment is now only through Pay.gov.
For DS-11: https://www.pay.gov/public/form/start/1274042472/
For DS-82: https://www.pay.gov/public/form/start/1156527186/
NOTE: No other forms of payment will be accepted. You must bring your proof of payment to the appointment at the Embassy or Consulate.
Wrong appointments:
Applicants who schedule an appointment for the wrong type of service will not be served (for example: the applicant schedules a passport appointment, but actually comes in for a Consular Report of Birth Abroad).
Late arrivals:
Late arrival will be asked to reschedule. Please allow 1 hour before your appointment time to take the Diplomatic Shuttle from 3rd Ave to the Embassy. A small fee applies for the Shuttle service.
Separate appointments:
Please schedule one separate appointment for each member of your family. For example, if you are a family of three, each with a passport renewal, please make three separate appointments.
Arrival at the Embassy:
The U.S. Embassy is located in the Diplomatic Enclave. All applicants must take a shuttle from the 3rd Avenue, Quaid-e-Azam University Road, Islamabad to the embassy. They offer several categories of service at several prices. (The shuttle service is a private company, and the price is subject to change without notice.). There is no parking near the Embassy’s Consular section.
Visiting the Embassy:
When visiting the Consular Section, U.S. Embassy, Islamabad, you must take the Diplomatic Shuttle. The shuttle stop is located at Third Avenue near Quaid-e-Azam University Road, G-5 Islamabad. The Diplomatic Shuttle will transport you from the Diplomatic Shuttle Service Center to the Consular Section for a small fee. Please ensure that you have some kind of identification with you, preferably your U.S passport. Other forms of identification may also be acceptable. If you need emergency service or have lost your passport, please inform security at the Diplomatic Shuttle Service Center of this and state that you are an American Citizen. Everyone entering the U.S. Embassy is subject to search. No luggage, electronic or photographic equipment is permitted in our waiting room or on Embassy premises.
U.S. Visas or Green Cards:
American citizen services staff MAY NOT answer visa questions. If you made an appointment for an American Citizen Service and use it to ask a visa question, you will be asked to return to the Shuttle bus. If you have a visa/green card inquiry click here! Please do not make an appointment with the ACS Unit.

Other Appointment Information
What to Bring
Everyone entering the Embassy/Consulate must bring a valid, government-issued I.D.
Prohibited Items
The following items are not allowed in the Embassy during your interview:
- Mobile phones
- Electronic personal digital assistants
- Cameras
- Audio or video recording devices
- Laptop computers
- Smart watches
- Bluetooth
- Portable music players
- Travel bags
- Back packs
- Briefcases
- Suitcases
- Cosmetics (including but not limited to spray perfume, cologne and talcum or baby powder)
- Bottles of any kind
- Food items
- Any sealed envelopes or packages.
Other items may be prohibited based on security staff discretion. There is no storage available for prohibited items. If you bring prohibited items with you, you will have to dispose of them before entry.
