Natalie A. Baker
Chargé d’Affaires
Natalie A. Baker, a Minister Counselor in the Senior Foreign Service, assumed the duties of Chargé d’Affaires ad interim (CDA) at the U.S. Embassy Islamabad in January 2025. She arrived in Islamabad as Deputy Chief of Mission in August 2024.
Stetson Sanders
Consul General, U.S. Consulate General Lahore
Stetson Sanders is the 35th U.S. Consul General in Lahore. This is his third diplomatic assignment relating to Pakistan. He served in U.S. Embassy Islamabad’s Management Section from 2019-2021 and also worked in Washington coordinating strategic management issues for Pakistan and central Asian countries.
Charles Goodman
Consul General, U.S. Consulate General Karachi
Charles Goodman assumed responsibility as the Consul General of U.S. Consulate General Karachi in August 2025.
He holds an MBA from Duke University and a B.S. from the University of Tampa, and joined the U.S. Foreign Service in 2006 after two decades in the private sector.
Thomas Eckert
Consul General, U.S. Consulate General Peshawar
Thomas Eckert, a career member of the Senior Foreign Service, has served in the Department of State since April 2003.
