U.S. University Fair, February 2026
ALL THE INFORMATION YOU NEED IN ONE PLACE!
Thinking about studying in the United States? Attend the EducationUSA University Fair in Karachi, Lahore or Islamabad to speak directly with admissions representatives from U.S. colleges and universities! EducationUSA is dedicated to helping students like you discover the numerous educational opportunities available in the United States. By connecting you with representatives from accredited U.S. colleges and universities, you can explore all the possibilities and discover the right fit for you!
This event is FREE to attend. EducationUSA is your Official Source on U.S. Higher Education and offers accurate, comprehensive and current information about opportunities to study at accredited postsecondary institutions in the United States.
