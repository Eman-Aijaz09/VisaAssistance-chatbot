Thomas Eckert, a career member of the Senior Foreign Service, has served in the Department of State since April 2003. Over the course of his 22 years, he has worked in Gabon, Vietnam, Burma, Cyprus and had two tours in Baghdad, Iraq, where he supervised all protection operations in 2011 and led the Baghdad Embassy Security Forces in 2019. Additionally, he volunteered for two temporary duty assignments in Kabul, Afghanistan, providing protection for then-President Hamid Karzai.
Most recently, Tom led an interagency team that supported the evacuation of U.S. citizens and Embassy staff across the Near East Region. This effort involved 150 military planners, ten federal agencies, and coordination with 26 other nations and impacted over 50,000 U.S. Citizens. The operation fostered international collaboration, supported U.S. Citizens in crisis, strengthened bilateral relationships, and advanced mission goals, including countering foreign malign influence and enhancing sanctions programs. Notably, Cyprus agreed to lead humanitarian assistance efforts in Gaza as part of this initiative.
Throughout his career, Eckert has demonstrated exceptional leadership and innovative problem-solving skills, managing large teams and complex operations under challenging circumstances. In Baghdad, he supervised over 900 personnel in the Embassy Security Forces during repeated attacks against the Embassy in 2019–2020. Eckert has also served temporarily as Chargé d’Affaires in Cyprus. He now serves as the Consul General at the U.S. Consulate in Peshawar, Pakistan. Originally from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, Tom is married and holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Political Science from Kutztown University.
