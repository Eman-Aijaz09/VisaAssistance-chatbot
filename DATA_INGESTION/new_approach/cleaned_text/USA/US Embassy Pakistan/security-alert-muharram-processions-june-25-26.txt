Location: Pakistan
Event: Security Alert
With the commencement of Muharram, the observance of Ashura started on June 17 and continues for 10 days, with the largest processions expected to take place between June 25-26. Many cities can expect major traffic congestion, diversions, and road closures during these times. Muharram processions have been targeted by militants for attacks in the past. Due to the possibility of violence, security services throughout the city will be on heightened alert.
We advise U.S. citizens in Pakistan to monitor local news, observe good personal security practices, including being aware of your surroundings, avoiding large crowds, and ensuring STEP registration is up to date.
Actions to Take:
- Avoid areas of large public gatherings.
- Review your personal security plans.
- Monitor local media for updates.
- Monitor email and local media for further updates regarding appointments at all locations.
- Keep a low profile.
- Carry identification and cooperate with police.
